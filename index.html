About this survey

The Brotosphere is a homing signal. Tell it what a place needs to have — bars, breweries, boat ramps, a golf course, a reasonable drive to an NFL stadium — and it shows you where in the country satisfies all of it. Not a ranking. Not a score out of 100. Just a map that says yes or no.

This build ships with synthetic sample data so the map works the moment you open it. The real pipeline (Overpass API pulls, H3 aggregation, monthly GitHub Action) lives in /etl and is documented in the README.
